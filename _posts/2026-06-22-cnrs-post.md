CNRS Competition
======

I am delighted to share that I have been admitted to the 2026 CNRS CRCN competition in Section 11 (No. 11/02). I am deeply grateful to everyone who has supported me throughout this journey, and I look forward to contributing to the scientific community through future research and collaborations.

This position is planned to be hosted by the Laboratory of Microstructure Studies and Mechanics of Materials (LEM3), Metz, France.

The semi-official results can be found here: [https://sncs.fr/2025/12/11/concours-cnrs-2026/#S11](https://sncs.fr/2025/12/11/concours-cnrs-2026/#S11)
