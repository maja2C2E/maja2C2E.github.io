# Welcome

**Maja Baniček Shaqfa**, Ph.D.

---

## About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Croatian Centre for Earthquake Engineering (CCEE) at the Faculty of Civil Engineering, University of Zagreb (Croatia).

I obtained my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from the University of Zagreb in 2022, with a dissertation on the influence of rail fastening clip geometry on the mechanical behaviour of fastening systems.

My research spans earthquake engineering, seismic assessment and retrofit of buildings, structural dynamics, masonry structures, and post-earthquake damage assessment. I also work on railway engineering topics including numerical modeling of track structures and rail fastening systems. My earlier work involved structural analysis methods such as graphic statics and the force density method.
